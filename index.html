About News Publications Service

I am a Ph.D. candidate at iSEE Lab, advised by Prof. Wei-Shi Zheng. I collaborated on research with the Biomimetics and Dextrous Manipulation Lab (BDML) at Stanford University.

Previously, I received my M.S. in control science and engineering from Southeast University, advised by Prof. Dan Niu, and my B.S. in automation from Northeastern University.

My research focuses on robotics AI, especially dexterous grasping and manipulation. I am also interested in humanoid robotics, MLLM-driven manipulation, and dexterous hand hardware.
